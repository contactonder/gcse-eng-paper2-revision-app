Universal Moves

Three syntactic power-ups. Any form.

DAREST vs. Syntactic Power-ups

Level 2 Ingredients vs Level 4 Engine

In class, DAREST stands for Direct address, Alliteration, Rhetorical questions, Emotive language, Statistics, Triples. That provides basic ingredients. Power-ups are the syntactic structures: where the emotive word sits, how the concession turns, and which punctuation mark secures Level 4 technical marks.

DAREST (Raw Ingredients)

Persuasive vocabulary: “Unacceptable”, “you/we”, a statistic, a triple list.

Power-ups (Level 4 Engine)

Place “Unacceptable.” as the minor sentence at the end of a 3-sentence rhythm stack for intentional cadence.

AO6 ACCURACY · POWER-UP 01

The Colon Rule

Examiners penalize essays that rely solely on commas and full stops. A colon demonstrates intentional, high-level grammatical control.

[Independent statement] : [direct clarification / cause]
TRIGGER:

In Paragraph 2 or 4 when expanding on a core problem or delivering an ultimate cause.

"The alternative is unthinkable: gridlock, toxic emissions, and total urban stagnation."
AO6 SYNTAX · POWER-UP 02

3-Sentence Rhythm Stack

Uniform sentence lengths sound repetitive. A long complex sentence, followed by a mid-length compound sentence, closed by a 1–3 word minor sentence creates dramatic cadence.

Complex (Long) → Compound (Mid) → Minor (1-3 words)
TRIGGER:

Paragraph 2 core problem breakdown, landing on an emphatic minor punch sentence.

"Although private cars offer individual freedom, they systematically destroy air quality. We commute in silence, yet we ignore the damage. Unacceptable."
AO5 COHESION · POWER-UP 03

The Pivot Bridge

Replaces simplistic markers like "On the other hand" with an integrated concession clause that immediately turns the argument back to your side.

Granted [opposing view]; nevertheless, [your refutation].
TRIGGER:

Paragraph 3 counter-argument refutation before dismantling opposition claims.

"Granted, car ownership provides undeniable convenience; nevertheless, our climate crisis demands immediate structural sacrifice."

AO5 Speech Tone, style and register are assuredly matched to purpose and audience. Address the hall immediately with a collective stance and a direct question. Greeting → audience stance → hook question
AO5 Article Tone, style and register are assuredly matched to publishing form. Show publication layout before the body text starts. Headline + strapline + byline + direct hook
AO5 Letter Tone, style and register are assuredly matched to formal recipient. Establish formal hierarchy and subject line before the argument. Recipient address + salutation + RE: subject
AO5 Leaflet Organises complex information logically with effective structural features. Chunk information into actionable sections with subheadings and bullet points. Title + subheading + action bullet points
AO5 Essay Sustained and inventive craft with controlled academic register. Open with an objective academic thesis statement. Thesis + conceptual signposting
AO5 Cohesion Fluently linked paragraphs with seamlessly integrated discourse markers. Avoid "Firstly / Secondly". Turn an opposing view smoothly using concessions. Granted [counter]; nevertheless, [refutation].
AO6 Punctuation Wide range of punctuation is used with a high level of accuracy. Use colons, semicolons, or dashes for structural clarification rather than decoration. [Statement] : [direct cause]
AO6 Sentences Uses a full range of appropriate sentence forms for effect. Vary sentence lengths systematically to control reading pace. Complex → compound → minor

AQA 8700/2 Level 4 Standard

Detailed breakdown of official descriptor bands for AO5 (24 marks) and AO6 (16 marks).

AO5 · Content and Organisation

24 marks total (60% of Section B)

19–24 MARKS

Upper Level 4 (22–24 marks) — Compelling

CONTENT:
  • Communication is convincing and compelling, directly relevant to focus and task.
  • Tone, style and register are assuredly matched to purpose and audience.
  • Extensive and ambitious vocabulary with sustained crafting of linguistic devices.
ORGANISATION:
  • Varied and inventive use of structural features.
  • Writing is compelling, incorporating a range of convincing and complex ideas.
  • Fluently linked paragraphs with seamlessly integrated discourse markers.

Lower Level 4 (19–21 marks) — Convincing

CONTENT:
  • Communication is convincing and directly relevant to focus and task.
  • Tone, style and register are convincingly matched to purpose and audience.
  • Extensive vocabulary with conscious crafting of linguistic devices.
ORGANISATION:
  • Varied and effective structural features.
  • Writing is highly engaging with a range of developed complex ideas.
  • Consistently coherent use of paragraphs with integrated discourse markers.

AO6 · Technical Accuracy

16 marks total (40% of Section B)

13–16 MARKS
  • Sentence Demarcation

    Sentence demarcation is consistently secure and consistently accurate across complex structures.

  • Punctuation Range

    Wide range of punctuation is used with a high level of accuracy (colons, semicolons, dashes).

  • Sentence Forms

    Uses a full range of appropriate sentence forms for intentional atmospheric effect.

  • Grammar & Spelling

    Standard English used consistently with high level of accuracy in ambitious vocabulary spelling.
